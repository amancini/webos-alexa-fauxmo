Alexa Fauxmo

Control this TV with Amazon Alexa — no hub, no cloud.

checking service…
"Alexa, turn TV on / off"screen on / off
"Alexa, set TV to 30%"volume
"Alexa, turn Netflix on"launch Netflix
"Alexa, turn Disney on"launch Disney+
"Alexa, turn Prime Video on"launch Prime Video
"Alexa, turn YouTube on"launch YouTube
"Alexa, set Channel to 5%"go to channel 5

First time: say "Alexa, discover devices". The service starts automatically on every boot. Edit ROOM in /var/lib/webosbrew/fauxmo/fauxmo.js to add a room name.
